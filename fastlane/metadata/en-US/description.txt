Athan Utility is a beautifully simple prayer times companion — precise, elegant, and completely private.

Built around a clean analog Athan clock and smooth gradient themes, it shows exactly what you need at a glance: your next prayer, a live countdown, and the full day's schedule. No clutter, no noise — just a calm, refined experience made for daily use.

PRIVACY FIRST
• No account, no sign-up, no tracking
• We never collect or sell your data
• Your location stays on your device and is used only to calculate accurate times

FEATURES
• Accurate prayer times for any location worldwide
• Smart calculation method — automatically suggests the right method for your country (Umm al-Qura, ISNA, Muslim World League, Karachi, Egyptian and more)
• Qibla compass pointing to the Kaaba
• Hijri (Islamic) calendar date
• Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets in every size
• NEW Extra Large widget with a live moon phase, current prayer, timer and full schedule
• Prayer reminder notifications with adjustable timing
• Custom prayer names and beautiful Athan sounds
• Elegant light and dark themes with customizable colors
• Fully optimized for iPhone and iPad
• Apple Watch complications and Siri support
