Version 8.0 is the biggest update Athan Utility has ever had.

• Native Mac app — a real macOS window with a sidebar, plus a menu bar item that counts down to the next prayer and opens a popover for quick changes
• Fajr Alarm — schedule a genuine iOS alarm locked to Fajr (or Sunrise) that follows the times as they shift through the year, with an offset for tahajjud or suhoor (requires iOS 26)
• Calendar integration — add months of prayer times to your calendar in one tap, and export the monthly view as a PDF or spreadsheet
• Rebuilt Apple Watch app, with complications for your watch face
• Widgets now appear in the Smart Stack around prayer times
• Ask Siri for the next prayer, or any prayer's time today
• A new Discover screen that walks you through everything the app can do

Now fully localized in 12 languages, with the prayer names and wording reviewed for each region.

Plus a long list of fixes and refinements throughout.
